Fix Country Profiles letter-jump bar scrolling behavior

The A-Z jump nav was set to position: sticky, pinning it near the top
of the viewport while the country list scrolled underneath it -
visually disconnecting it from the content it's meant to navigate.

- Remove position: sticky (and its top/z-index/background offset) from
  .country-letter-jump so it's a normal in-flow element again: it now
  scrolls together with the country list and scrolls out of view like
  everything else once you pass it, instead of staying pinned.
- Reduce .country-letter-section's scroll-margin-top from
  navbar-height + 56px to navbar-height + 16px, since a jump-to-letter
  click only needs to clear the (still sticky) top navbar now, not the
  letter bar's own height.
