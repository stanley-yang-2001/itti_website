Restore Publish nav-link removal and homepage icons, fix watermark bug,
move Facebook link to homepage Connect section

Note: a prior commit on this branch reverted the Publish-link removal
and homepage icon/watermark work (Home.jsx, App.css, NavBar.jsx reverted
to their pre-existing state, likely from a stale local copy being
committed over them) - this restores that work and adds today's fixes
on top.

- NavBar.jsx: remove the 'Publish' link and its isPublisher check -
  it pointed at the older standalone /publish page, made redundant by
  the newer Profile > Publications report-upload workflow
- Home.jsx: add line-art icons to the four Explore cards, drawn from
  ITTI's own seal (globe/graticule, laurel branch) rather than generic
  icon-library substitutes - a pediment/column glyph for About
  (governance/institutional structure), a laurel branch for Fellowship
  (echoing the seal's wreath) rather than a generic 'people' icon
- Home.jsx: add a faint background watermark (the same globe/graticule
  motif) behind the Welcome hero
- Home.jsx: revise two card descriptions that had drifted toward
  casual marketing language, to match the site's own established
  register (e.g. Fellowship's 'invitation-only global fellowship
  developing leaders for trauma-informed nations')
- App.css: icon styling kept deliberately restrained - opacity change
  on hover only, no lift/bounce motion, consistent with this being a
  nonprofit research institute rather than a consumer product
- App.css: fix the Welcome hero's watermark rendering as a large,
  out-of-place globe icon above the message instead of a faint
  centered backdrop. '.home-welcome > *{position:relative}' had the
  same specificity as, and came after, the watermark's own
  'position:absolute', silently overriding it and knocking the SVG
  into normal document flow at full size. Excluded the watermark from
  that rule with :not(.home-welcome-watermark).
- Home.jsx: add a third Connect-section card for ITTI's Facebook page,
  matching the existing YouTube/Outlets for Hope card style. Widened
  .home-connect-grid's max-width (800px -> 1080px) so three cards sit
  comfortably in one row on desktop.
- Footer.jsx: remove the now-redundant Facebook link from
  SOCIAL_LINKS (and the constant itself, now unused) - it lives on
  the homepage instead, consistent with how YouTube/OFH aren't
  duplicated in the footer either.
- README.md: note Facebook alongside YouTube/OFH in the #connect
  section description.
